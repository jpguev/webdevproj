Waste Recycling Guide

Matutong mag tapon ng basura nang tama at maging responsable sa ating kapaligiran. Ang tamang paghihiwalay ng basura ay makakatulong sa pagbawas ng polusyon, pag-save ng resources, and to protect our environment.

Compost

Separate fruit and vegetable scraps, coffee grounds, and yard waste for a home or community compost program where available.

Recycle

Check local rules for accepted paper, cardboard, glass, metal, and plastic. Empty and rinse containers, then keep materials dry.

Reuse first

Choose reusable bags, bottles, and containers. Repair, refill, donate, or repurpose items before throwing them away.

Special waste

Keep batteries, electronics, bulbs, chemicals, and medical sharps out of regular bins. Use designated local drop-off or collection services.

Quick tip: When unsure, check your local waste authority’s guidance. A contaminated item can make a whole batch harder to recycle.
